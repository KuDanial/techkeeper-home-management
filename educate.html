Maintenance

LG Dishwasher

Clean the cylindrical filter

Regularly cleaning your dishwasher filter prevents clogs, ensures sparkling dishes, and protects the pump from debris build-up.
REQUIRED CONSUMABLES

Warm water, mild dish soap, soft brush.

RECOMMENDED TOOLS & EQUIPMENT

Small cleaning brush or old toothbrush.

1: Remove bottom rack

Pull the bottom rack all the way out or remove it completely to access the floor of the dishwasher.

2: Unlock the filter

Turn the cylindrical filter counter-clockwise and lift it straight up out of the dishwasher floor.

3: Rinse and clean

Rinse the filter under warm running water. Use a soft brush to remove any trapped food particles.
